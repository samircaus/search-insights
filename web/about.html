Search Insights

Search Insights for Experience Workspace

Search Insights is a side panel for Adobe Experience Workspace. While you edit a page, it shows how that page performs in Google Search: clicks, impressions, click-through rate, average position, the top queries, and the indexing status from Google's URL Inspection.

You pick the queries and checks that matter, preview a short brief, and then copy it, insert it into the document after confirming, or draft a prompt for the Workspace AI assistant. Nothing is sent or changed without a click from you.

How it works

You connect your own Google account with read-only Search Console access. The panel only reads data for properties your Google account can already see in Search Console, and only for pages on sites configured by the operator.

Who runs it
